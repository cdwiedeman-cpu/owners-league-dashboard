Getting Started

Everything you need to open the league, put it on your phone, and enter a trade. It takes about five minutes once.

The Address

This is the whole thing. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

rflowners.com

Your Code

Bob emails you a six character code, on its own, just for you. It is how the site knows which eleven teams are yours.

Type it into the box the first time you open the league, and press Sign Me In. Upper case and lower case both work. That device then stays signed in for the rest of the season.

Two characters catch people out. No code has the letter O in it and no code has the number 1 in it. So a character that looks like the letter O is a zero, and one that looks like the number 1 is the letter I.

Your code is yours alone. Anybody who has it can enter trades for your team, so please do not pass it on. If it will not work, email Bob and he will send you a new one.

Every Device Asks Once

The league remembers each device on its own. So your computer asks for the code once, your phone asks once, and the home screen icon asks once. After that it never asks again.

That is not a fault. It is what stops somebody who picks up your phone from being asked nothing at all.

Put It On Your Phone

This is the part that makes it an app. You get an icon that sits with your other apps and opens straight to the league. It takes about two minutes. The steps are different on each kind of phone, so find yours.

1

Open the league in Safari.

Go to rflowners.com.

2

Find the Share button.

It is a square with an arrow coming out of the top of it.

On most iPhones it sits along the bar at the foot of the screen. On newer iPhones that bar is gone: tap the three dots beside the web address instead, then tap Share.

Tap Add to Home Screen.

It is some way down that list, so keep scrolling.

Leave Open as Web App turned on.

If you see that switch, leave it on. It is what makes the league open like an app rather than a browser tab.

5

Tap Add.

The icon appears with your other apps, labelled RFL.

What the Icon Looks Like

Your phone puts it wherever it puts any new app, which on most phones is the last page of the home screen. Press and hold it to drag it somewhere you will find it.

Tapping it opens the league on its own, with no address bar and no browser tabs around it. It looks and behaves like any other app on your phone.

Open it from the icon and type your code in one more time. The icon keeps its own sign-in, separate from your browser, so it asks once and then never again.

How to Enter a Trade

1

Tap the Trades tab, then Add a Trade.

2

Box 1 is The Team You Want.

Start typing a team name and the list narrows as you type. Teams you are not allowed to claim are still shown, dimmed, with the reason written beside them.

3

Box 2 is What You Would Give Up.

Choose up to four of your own teams you would be willing to lose for it, in the order you would rather lose them.

Only one of them is actually dropped. It is the first team on your list that you still hold and are still allowed to drop. The teams under it are the next ones in your order. One of those is dropped instead if an earlier trade on your list has already taken your first choice.

4

Want more than one team? Tap Add Another Trade.

You can enter up to ten. They are taken in order, top to bottom, so put what you want most at the top. The button is under the last trade on your list, which is the one you have just finished.

You cannot slot a trade into the middle of the list. The order decides which team is still yours to drop by the time a later trade runs, so moving one would quietly change the others. To fix a trade in the middle, tap Remove Trade on it and enter it again.

5

From the second trade on, choose When to Try This Trade.

Three choices, and each one names the trade it waits on.

  • Try It No Matter What is the normal one. Most trades are this.
  • Only if I Win Trade 1 is how you say “if I do get Navy, go after Alabama as well”.
  • Only if I Miss Trade 1 is how you say “if I miss out on Navy, try Alabama instead”.

The trade number changes to whichever earlier trade you point it at.

6

Want a backup? Tap Add a Backup Trade.

A backup trade is one that is only tried if the trade above it does not go through. The button builds it for you in one press, already pointing at the trade above it. You still choose the team you want and the teams you would give up.

Missing a trade means missing it for any reason. Losing the coin flip counts, and so does running out of teams to drop.

The trade says what it is at the top of its own card, so you can tell a backup from an ordinary trade without opening anything.

7

Press Submit My Trades.

Nothing counts until you press it. You can change your list and submit again as often as you like, right up to the deadline. Nobody else sees what you have entered.

The three buttons at the top of the Trades tab are worth a read before your first one. How to Enter a Trade walks through it. What Am I Allowed to Trade? lists the rules on holding and claiming. How the Week Works explains the two windows.

When Trades Happen

Every time in the league is Arizona time, all year round.

Monday, 3:59pm AZThe last minute to get your list in.
Monday, 4:00pm AZ onwardsBob runs the coin flips. If two owners in the same division want the same team, this is what settles it. Nothing can settle before 4:00pm, and he runs them as soon as he can after that.

Later in the season the site will settle them by itself, at 4:00pm on the dot. Bob will tell you before that happens.
Until Tuesday, 5:00pm AZFirst come, first served. Anything in your division that nobody owns is yours if you ask for it first. No queue and no coin flip. The Trades tab changes to a Claim a Team form by itself when this window opens.

The Rules, In Short

  • One team in and one team out, every time.
  • College for college, NFL for NFL. FBS and FCS both count as college.
  • You have to hold a team for three of its games before you can drop it.
  • A team you pick up has to have three games still to play.
  • A team whose season is over cannot be traded away at all.

The site already applies all of this. It will not let you build a trade that breaks a rule, and it says why beside any team you cannot pick.

If You Get Stuck

Email Bob. Send him the trade you want in whatever words you like and he will enter it for you, then get you set up to do the next one yourself.

And if anything on the site looks wrong, please say so, even if you are not sure.
